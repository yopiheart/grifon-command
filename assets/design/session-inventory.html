// Not done — I parked these on you

Six things I did not change

Each one contradicts something already written into DESIGN.md or your brand decisions. That's why I held them — but you approved repeatedly and I kept them buried at the bottom of long replies instead of putting them in front of you. That part was wrong.

01
Monochrome rail vs 15 identity hues

Apple prefers monochromatic toolbars over colourful backgrounds. Your rail now has 15 module hues on glass over a gold field. But per-module hues are a documented Grifon decision — this is identity, not a defect.

Keep the hues, go monochrome, or desaturate them ~40% on the rail only?

02
Legal links are 16–17px tall

Terms, Privacy, Refund, Support in the footers. Wide enough to hit, too short vertically. Fixing means giving those rows real height, which changes how the footer looks.

Want me to restructure the footer rows?

03
Boot screen makes you wait

It enforces a minimum hold after the session is already ready. Apple's rule is: don't make people wait for an animation. But that hold is your film-leader beat working as designed — it's brand, not bug.

Keep the beat, or let it dismiss the moment the session is ready?

04
Pricing section edges still don't align

Landing sections all start at x=0 now. Pricing doesn't — each section centres at its own width, so the left edges land at three different places. Fixing it means restructuring a 1000-line file built with inline styles.

Worth the refactor, or leave pricing as-is?

05
Concentric radii — helper only

The rule is: a nested corner equals the outer radius minus the padding, so curves stay parallel. I wrote concentricRadius() but never applied it anywhere. It's a real polish pass across many components.

Want me to sweep it?

06
Six desktop lines near the top of the band

Around 69 real characters — inside the 45–75 readable range, just at its upper edge. I fixed the two genuine outliers (127ch and 105ch) and left these, because tightening them is taste, not a defect.

Leave them, or cap everything at 65ch?
