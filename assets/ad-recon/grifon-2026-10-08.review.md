## Your Offer, Less Blank Page

Review readiness: usable with explicit review of limits.
Format: UGC
Customer problem: The owner has a clear product and offer but loses momentum deciding what to say in an ad.
Selling angle: Turn a concrete product and offer into a reviewable starting plan that the owner can correct before production.
Hook idea: Your product is ready. Your offer is ready. Your ad is still a blank page.
Original proof plan: An identified demonstrator walks through a real product/offer and the resulting plan in the existing flow. Show one useful correction before creative production. Treat the demonstrator as a walkthrough, not a customer testimonial. Any elapsed-time comparison requires a measured capture; omit agency prices and invented savings.
Required assets: Owned product and offer details; Real input-to-plan screen capture; A consenting demonstrator or clearly identified AI presenter; One documented review correction; Approved cold-traffic CTA: Try It Free; free access is an ad plan, not a promised free finished video
Test hypothesis: Priority 3; problem-aware. Naming the blank-page task may bring in owners who have not yet chosen an AI tool. Creatify's agency-friction framing is observed, while Philippine SMB response is untested. Measure completed qualified free plans and subsequent activation; do not optimize solely for low-cost clicks.
