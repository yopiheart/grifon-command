The creative decision

Lead with recognition; earn the premium reveal. The owner and product give the viewer something concrete to follow. The living studio and accepted gold ribbons connect the work. Actual Grifon exports carry the quality claim. The existing studio candidate remains preserved as look development, subordinate to the story.

Reference transfer: the saved GPT/Astra study informs idea-to-output progression; the Apple/iPhone study informs disciplined presentation. S20 supports real interface as proof. These are creative inputs, not evidence of conversion lift, video pacing or completed AAA quality. No new research or paid calls were made for this board.

Quality bar: consistent bottle/label/fill, believable person and materials, readable mobile captures, purposeful motion and sound, original proof exports and reproducible app results. No old samples, fabricated testimonials, voice-controlled UI or instant-generation promise.

Still provisional: exact owner/brand, dialogue, duration, shot timings, music and sound cues. The earlier audiovisual study did not pass its specificity gate. Natural Taglish speech with English UI and subtitles remains the direction; exact lines need review. Premium/AAA is the ambition, not a delivery claim.

Approved production order: Storyboard → rough timed edit with temporary audio → produce individual shots with the appropriate tools → assemble and review the final film → separately approved website integration. Rough timing is an editorial test, not a locked runtime or cleared audiovisual gate. Blender is optional for selected shots; a rendered frame or camera preview may guide a compatible video model for that shot. It does not replace this sequence with a Blender-first whole-film pipeline.

Before production: approve the new product and casting assets, complete the audiovisual brief, and approve exact bounded proof-generation tests. Review originals before locking the cut. Later release checks cover mute comprehension, real mobile playback and the free-plan journey. No render, paid generation, merge or deployment is authorized by this storyboard.

Proposed disclosure near playback: Demo brand. Sample ads made in Grifon on a paid plan. Studio scenes produced separately.
