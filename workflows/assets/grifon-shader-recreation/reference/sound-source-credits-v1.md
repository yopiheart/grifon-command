pan, contact frames, gain, ducking and mastering are recorded in `sound-spec.json`
and its verification receipts. The old pre-subscription ElevenLabs recordings
are not used in this version.

listed web/social video and online advertisements as supported uses, and the
official [sound-effects FAQ](https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/) described
commercial and personal video use. Their item-specific
[license](https://mixkit.co/license/) remains the governing source. This note
does not extend those permissions to stock redistribution or a software library.

The broad measured dynamics fit informed the music choice. It does not prove
heard rhythm, exact song identity, subjective quality or superiority to a
competitor. The user's listening judgment remains the final taste check.
