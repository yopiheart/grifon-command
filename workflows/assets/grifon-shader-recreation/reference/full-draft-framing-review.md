# Historical continuation draft framing feedback

Before full encoding, the parent reviewed 17 corresponding source/draft
positions on five preserved boards in `../qa/full-draft-framing-root/`.
This was early framing feedback on a mutable draft, not a frozen full-film
native or actual-playback gate. The board receipt records that limitation.

The parent found these structural gaps and held final encoding:

- Frame242 aperture lacked the source's substantial negative center and short
  segmented dotted blades.
- Frame324 lens appeared as a bounded oval over black corners instead of the
  source's enormous cropped field and lower curved rim.
- Frames362/383/393 substituted stacked closed lobes, isolated cups and flat
  wedges for the source's open twisting/folded shells and curved pinwheel.
- Fast pattern treatments needed dense grain and distinct material detail.
- Frame476 glyph had broad black margins instead of a cropped warm macro.
- Frame529 mobile view needed the continuing genuine desktop/editor backdrop;
  the draft exposed the later binary field too early.
- Frames617/633 used a floating plain poster with a detached caption instead of
  a full binary/colored-panel composition and a caption within the pulling-back
  panel.
- Frames740/842 needed organic contours and the shared end composition's
  tilt/recession/fade, rather than regular waves and a persistently bright mark.
- Frame285 catalog labels were too small and thumbnails too uniform.

The producer acknowledged these as structural gaps and began corrections.
Correctness of those revisions is decided by later bound full-film reviews,
not by this feedback or the passing opening gate. English copy, canonical
Grifon mark and actual product-capture provenance were retained. No paid call,
new generation model, audio acceptance or frozen opening mutation occurred.
