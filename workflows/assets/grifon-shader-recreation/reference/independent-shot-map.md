# Independent reference shot map

Reference-only visual analysis, 2026-10-08. This is a close-recreation brief, not a verdict on the earlier Grifon launch. No old story, scene order or pacing is treated as approved. No paid tool, browser/GPU capture, install or production-source edit was used by this reviewer.

Source: https://x.com/npm_i_shaders/status/2107467668009963819. Local source: `reference/source-x.mp4`, 11,514,975 bytes; SHA-256 `47c8bbc8e3d06953dd850500e4c7702ce690b3a4bc6708c4fc6a1f3d392df089`. Independently probed: 1920x1080, H.264 yuv420p, 932 video frames at 30000/1001 fps; video extent 31.097733s. Stereo AAC 48 kHz and container extent 31.146667s. The source has an audio stream, but this reviewer has not heard it. Sound, speech, music, beat alignment and sonic transitions remain unassessed.

Method: independently decoded all 932 source frames to `.tmp/grifon-reference-review/all/`, inspected a complete beginning-to-end quarter-second strip, all frames of the ring montage, additional exact-frame strips around the opening/middle/editor/end, before/after pairs at scene-score candidates, and seven full-resolution key frames. FFmpeg scene score >0.08 supplied candidates; candidates caused by motion, palette animation or glitch are not automatically counted as cuts. Frame indices below are zero-based; time = frame * 1001/30000. Ranges are half-open. Named hard-cut boundaries were read on decoded adjacent frames. Dissolves, deformation transitions and semantic phases are identified separately. This map does not claim a unique shot count for the continuous effects.

All observed imagery is synthetic authored motion or interface footage. There is no human subject/performance, so human believability dimensions are not applicable. Apparent glass, metal, raised lettering, camera pitch and depth are visual observations; the generating implementation, lens parameters, exact font, actual 3D geometry and GPU engine cannot be proved from exported pixels. No performance or winner claim is made.

## Framing, typography and repeat requirements

- At frame84 /2.803s the ring's outer shape occupies approximately x24.5%-75.5%, y27.5%-72.5%; it is centered with a large horizontal opening and thick rounded band.
