# Opening pilot implementation handoff

This is a silent visual comparison pilot through frame222, not a completed film
or a reference-fidelity pass. Existing launch films and exports were preserved.

## Files

- `pilot.json`: one editable copy/cut/camera/framing source, matching the measured
  frame boundaries. Grifon substitutions are the two exact English phrases in
  the approved brief and gold/cream/charcoal/near-white material variations.
- `build-pilot.mjs`: local normalized Inter contours, even-odd glyph masks,
  Euclidean signed-distance generation, vendor/license copy and asset receipt.
- `assets/`: two headerless512x512Float32 fields, masks, license and manifest.
- `vendor/`: unchanged already-installed Three r180 distribution and license.
- `pilot.html`, `pilot.js`, `surfaces.frag`: a GPU canvas with refractive lens,
  title projection/pitch, open bowl reversal, shared analytic capsule-ring shape
  under eight treatments, flare, distance-field raised lettering and corner relief.
  Every material cut and the title camera keyframes are read from `pilot.json`.
- `render-pilot.mjs`: private allowlisted HTTP composition, blocked external
  requests, local hardware capture, fresh/nonlinear replay, frame hash receipt,
  silent H264 encoding and strict decode/probe verification.
- `verify-sdf.mjs`: all-pixel sign checks, font-counter reconstruction, independent
  brute-force distance checks, and rejection of deliberately bad binary fixtures.
- `qa/`: technical receipts, sample images, contact sheet and preserved pilot-v1.
- `grifon-opening-pilot-silent.mp4`: current222-frame silent comparison output.
