## Ideation review

Audience: a business owner who knows their product but needs help turning it
into an ad. Awareness: solution aware. English launch-film register continues
the approved pilot direction; this is not a Taglish direct-response ad.

Three routes considered:

1. **Worth seeing — selected.** A product becomes a specific angle, script and
   visual sequence. One recognizable asset persists. Mechanism transparency
   addresses the 'generic AI output' objection. S20/S32 in the Static Swipe
   File support product/process specificity; E19 K04/K05 inform motion only.
2. **A room full of ideas.** Many floating concepts converge into one. Visually
   flexible, but likely to repeat the prior pilot's abstract generic messaging.
3. **One sentence, many worlds.** The same hook animates through several visual
   styles. Strong craft showcase, but distracts from what Grifon does for a business.

Single takeaway: Grifon helps turn product knowledge into a planned ad story.
No sales lift, guaranteed speed, autonomous publishing or free-video claim.
Feature-copy source: `config/planFeatures.ts` lists research, script, storyboard
and finished ads. That is repository evidence, not a fresh live runtime test.
