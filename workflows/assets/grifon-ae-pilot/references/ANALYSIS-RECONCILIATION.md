## Implications for our actual pilot

Inspection of build-grifon-launch.jsx exposes a concrete limitation: one shared
text helper and one 72-percent temporal influence serve many different beats.
Many text layers exit through the same vertical movement and opacity fade. The
camera changes distance around one central target. A camera-layer/key count is
therefore technical evidence only, not proof of expressive choreography.

The next creative revision needs distinct transition behavior per narrative
purpose. A selected brief could persist into its angle, then become a script
container, then a sequence; each transformation needs a readable before/after.

reading time, foreground/background movement and arrival/settle separately.
