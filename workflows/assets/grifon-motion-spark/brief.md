# Grifon / Spark — 15-second motion study

Purpose: introduce Grifon to business owners who find it hard to start content.
Neil approved the proposed idea-card → arranged creative cards → Grifon reveal
direction after supplying an Apple ad and a longer YouTube reference.
This is an authored motion study, not footage of Grifon's app or proof of output.

Exact visible copy: `DESCRIBE`, `BUILD`, `LAUNCH`, `GRIFON`, `GRIFON.AI`.
Reuse the previously approved words; no new offer, pricing, or performance claim.
15 seconds, 24 fps; portrait 1080x1920 and separately composed landscape 1920x1080.
No voiceover. Existing local music; no Apple footage or soundtrack in the export.

One visual idea: a gold spark wakes a blank idea card, carries its contents into
three arranged creative panels, then leads the eye into the canonical brand mark.

| Time | Image and motion | Reading/hold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3.2 | Close angled glass card; light crosses its edge; DESCRIBE settles into focus | Word readable from 0.8s |
| 3.2–5 | Card pulls away; three panels emerge from its center with an overlapping handoff | Transition only |
| 5–8.5 | BUILD above script/image/video graphic panels; strokes fill in order | Word and panel structure hold |

| 11.5–15 | Motion resolves into GrifonMark and GRIFON.AI; particles settle | Complete mark readable by 12.5s, held to end |

Assets: public/logo.png (unchanged inside thin transparent gold ring), existing

## Reference observations and limits

- Canonical short ad: https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1675348771268565
- Longer supplied reference: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DDnNc60onT4
- YouTube player displayed 54 seconds; sampled manually through its visible UI.
- Approx. 1s: narrow overhead light in a dark field; 7s: close product/camera view
  in vapor; 17s: internal hardware fills the frame with centered benefit text;
  27s: extreme lens close-up and a short centered line; 37s: live-action camera
  view; 47s: isolated brand mark. These are sampled observations, not exact cuts.
- Short-ad samples: dark scene around 5s, illuminated particles and short text
  around 9s, product/name closing. The longer video is not assumed frame-identical.
- Borrow macro scale, light-led reveals, brief centered typography, contrast in
  scale, and a quiet brand ending. Adapt these to authored Grifon geometry.
- Audio was not listened to through the browser tools; visible captions are not
  an audio audit. No paid analyzer, transcript tool, or pattern-count update.

Success: both layouts have readable exact text, canonical mark, visible motion,
clean holds, no unintended blank transition, full 15s decode and unclipped audio.
Failure: illegible text, logo distortion, overlaps, timing drift, audio clipping,
or implying the graphic panels are actual app output. Verify sampled transitions,
phone-size frame sheets, FFprobe/FFmpeg and an isolated text revision.

Delivery is a first creative preview; Neil's feedback and subjective playback/
listening remain separate from machine verification. No paid or external publish.
