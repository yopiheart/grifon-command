## Asset decisions and conflicts

1. **Product evidence first.** `node tools/codex-assets.mjs wizard` reports 44 tracked inputs, zero absent. Matching screenshots for the chosen saved project have not been established; this is an asset/capture task, not a reported app defect. Do not rebuild fake UI to fill the gap.
2. **Existing samples are useful but not flawless.** `recipes.ts` identifies the Hiraya Glow source run as `apr_34d06c7f`; the shipped short excerpt avoids a defective earlier morph in the full ad. It is a silent 9.03-second tile excerpt, not an audio-bearing complete ad. The proposed seven-second proof window requires selecting a coherent shorter passage or extending that beat and revising the 25-second total. Do not speed it up or cut its payoff merely to fit. Preserve burned captions; use separately reviewed music/SFX, or recover a corresponding full-source audio segment if that becomes the chosen approach. The Bukid Coffee unboxing sample has a documented box sub-label defect. Prefer reviewing the shipped Hiraya excerpt before choosing footage; do not feature either entire sample uncritically.
3. **One project's story needs matching inputs and outputs.** Do not splice an unrelated product upload, invented Script, and a shipped sample into apparent end-to-end proof. If matching saved data cannot be recovered, make an honestly labeled sample showcase instead; keep the workflow scenes pending.
4. **Brand identity stays Grifon's.** Charcoal `#09090B`, gold `#FFCC00`, unchanged white griffin inside the transparent thin gold circle, Inter Black italic `GRIFON`. No Apple mark, device claims, branded multicolor assistant treatment or music in the export.
5. **Readable motion beats dim text.** Borrow the traveling emphasis, but keep the complete line readable; direct imitation of nearly black type would fail phone readability. Use smaller perspective angles for UI than for abstract product shots.
6. **Current music is a candidate, not a match.** The previous demo's track passing loudness checks does not establish that it suits this reference. Choose and listen to the new mix before calling it finished.

The screen map uses existing UI labels and the approved brand/URL. Additional marketing headlines are not locked here. If new Taglish headlines are used, present their exact wording for the repository's copy approval before burning them into the preview.
