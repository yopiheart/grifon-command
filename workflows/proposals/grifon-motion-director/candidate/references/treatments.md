# Three starting treatments

These are composition starting points, not validated provider presets. Select by the communication problem; preserve the client's brand and approved copy.

## Product or UI launch

Use when the viewer needs to understand an idea becoming a result. Carry one card or graphic shape through reveal, input, arrangement, and brand ending. Keep the main word or interaction readable before the next movement. Use actual screenshots for genuine UI proof; use abstract panels only for a clearly identified motion sample.

The 12-second Grifon demo uses four approved beats: logo reveal (0-3s), DESCRIBE in a prompt card (3-6s), BUILD with arranged panels (6-9s), LAUNCH leading to logo and GRIFON.AI (9-12s). It has music and no voiceover. This is a demo specification, not the default duration/copy for other projects.

Portrait can stack the headline above a large central card; landscape can place the headline beside a wider card arrangement. Preserve the story and timing while composing both separately.

## Talking-head graphics

Use when the speaker needs one supporting idea made visible. Work with approved clean footage. Place a short authored label, illustrative icon, or UI card alongside the speaker, timed to the actual phrase. Avoid faces, mouths, and existing captions. Use the smallest graphic set that adds meaning; ordinary cutting and voice/audio repair remain with AI Editor.

Keep footage timing and identity intact unless editing is explicitly requested. If a caption transcript or timing is missing, do not invent speech or invoke paid transcription silently. A portrait layout can use safe space above/beside the speaker; landscape can use an adjacent graphic column.

## Client product or service promo

Use when the hero is a supplied product photograph, service image, or approved brand mark. Reveal the hero, introduce one approved benefit, then settle into the supplied CTA. Keep product geometry and branding unchanged; use authored shapes and light accents rather than fabricated product views or unsupported before/after claims.

Start with the client's design rules and asset paths. Do not import Grifon's palette or historical offers. Request a missing identity asset instead of generating a substitute. AI-generated imagery is an optional separately approved input, not required for this treatment.
