# Grifon-Starter Funnel — Lead Magnet Options (Neil Gate #1)

**Decision needed:** pick the lead magnet for the first Funnel Forge dogfood funnel
(`funnels/grifon-starter/`). Free magnet → email nurture → **Starter** bridge
(priority ladder #4, feeding #3 cold signups). Per the KX funnel lens, every option
below is designed to push the lead to *ship something with Grifon* — the magnet is

## Option A — "Libreng Ad Battle Plan" (RECOMMENDED, 65% confidence)

**The offer:** *"Sagutin mo ang 4 na tanong. Makukuha mo ang free Ad Battle Plan para
sa negosyo mo — anong hook ang gagana sa niche mo, anong angle ang sinusunod ng mga
nanalong ads ngayon, at ang unang ad na dapat mong i-run — in under 60 seconds."*

**Anatomy (archetype: `minimal`, quiz-front):** F04's quiz-as-magnet pattern verbatim —
one-question-per-screen (niche → monthly ad budget band → biggest problema → email),
personalized-feeling output. Fulfillment trick: **L2 audience-skinning applied to the
magnet itself** — we pre-build 8–10 niche variants of the Battle Plan (food, fashion,
services, e-com, etc.) with Grifon's own Market Research + Caption & Headline modules,
so "personalized" = niche-routed, zero per-lead ops. The plan's last page IS the
Starter bridge: "Gusto mo bang gawin ni Grifon ang ad na 'to para sa'yo? Yung Battle
Plan mo, i-paste mo lang."

**Why it wins:** Reciprocity + Commitment (the quiz answers are micro-commitments —
L3, 5 corroborations across 2 operators) + personalization beats generic (F04:
"Personalized $100M Scaling Roadmap…in under 30 seconds", 250k requested). Awareness
stage: **Problem Aware** — Bryan knows his ads don't convert; the magnet names WHY sa
niche niya. Thank-you page follows L1 (`proposed`, 6/6): deliver + one bridge CTA to
create a free Grifon account and generate the first ad. Quiz answers land in
`funnel_leads.utm.qualifiers` = lead scoring from day one.

**Cost to ship:** ~1 day (8–10 niche PDFs/pages generated with our own modules —
dogfooding proof din). **Risk:** "battle plan" must be genuinely good or the Starter
bridge dies — quality gate ako (CMO) bago mag-live.

## Option B — "50 Patunay na Hooks" swipe pack (fast + safe)

**The offer:** *"50 hooks mula sa mga ads na talagang gumana — decoded, hindi hula.
Kopyahin mo, palitan mo ng brand mo, i-run mo ngayong linggo."* Sourced from our own
swipe files (93 static entries, S-patterns na may ≥3 corroborations lang ang isasama).

**Anatomy (archetype: `minimal`):** F03's pure form — headline + mockup + email + 1
qualifier (niche). Zero scroll persuasion; the ad does the selling (F03 doctrine).

**Why it works:** Reciprocity + Authority ("decoded from winning ads" — totoo, and we
can say how many we analyzed: real numbers per L9, 4 across 3 operators). Awareness:
**Solution Aware**. Cheapest, shippable in half a day, evergreen.
**Why it's #2:** walang commitment mechanism, walang personalization — magnet
consumers ≠ product users; mas mahina ang KX bridge to actually shipping an ad.
Confidence: 55%.

## Option C — "7 Ads in 7 Days" challenge (bold/experimental)

**The offer:** free 7-day challenge — one ad a day gamit ang Grifon free tier, Viber/
Discord group, si Neil live twice. F02's challenge anatomy (segmentation quiz →
registration → daily consumption → paid bridge), minus the trust flags (walang fake
seat counter, walang "$97→FREE" na may tago — L5 is do-not-replicate).

**Why it could win big:** THE strongest KX play — by day 7 they've shipped 7 assets;
Commitment + Liking + real community. Product-usage-first exactly as the lens demands.
**Why it's risky:** heavy ops (daily content + moderation + live), cohort scheduling
kills the evergreen funnel MVP, and challenge funnels need warm traffic we haven't
built. Confidence today: 35% — **park it as the Phase-2 play** once the evergreen
funnel proves the offer.

**My call:** **A**, with B as the fallback kung gusto mo mag-ship bukas. A converts
better (commitment + personalization), scores leads from the first touch, at ang
fulfillment ay dogfooding mismo ng product. C is a great second act, wrong first act.
