## Why these devices may help

These are interpretations, not verified creator intent or retention findings:

- A gray, faceless body removes facial detail, while saturated blue equipment
  separates the main performer from the darker trainer and background.
- Whole-body framing preserves the relationship among stance, rotation, glove,
  and pad. Random close-ups would make the technique harder to follow.
- The action label appears in nearby empty space rather than on the moving
  gloves. Its job is identification, not transcribing spoken sentences.
- A small progress indicator makes the sequence finite. The final list converts
  the performed actions into something viewers can remember or practice.
- The caption points to an app waitlist. Several visible comments identify a
  recognizable boxer/style, including through sound. Existing interest and
  recognition are material confounders; the rendering is not a sufficient
  explanation for engagement.

Exact font and letter spacing were not identified. The source's condensed,
upper-left type and pure-black backdrop do not override Neil's approved centered
white Inter treatment or Oceanides navy. Nor does an eight-step sports sequence
establish a useful reading speed for a general local-service audience.

## Decision for Oceanides

Build one original **3D mechanism demonstration**: ordinary flow, a restriction,
then upstream water accumulation. Keep the isolated subject, limited palette,
continuous action, timed identification, and clear progression. Author three
states over eight seconds, followed by a three-second contact hold.

The source's specific athlete, movements, footage, and audio are not production
inputs. A pipe mechanism is more relevant to this service audience than copying
the faceless boxer. Normal-body human motion is not needed for this adaptation.

Use local Blender for editable geometry, contained water levels, obstruction,
lighting, and camera; use the existing HTML/FFmpeg approach for accurate text and
the approved transparent logo. This choice prioritizes control over the mechanism.
It does not establish that code is always sufficient for people, fluids, or every
Glovio-style video. This pilot uses authored water geometry, not a fluid solver.

The possible mechanism is grounded in [Washington Department of Health's
description of a clogged house-to-tank pipe and possible backup](https://doh.wa.gov/community-and-environment/wastewater-management/septic-system/signs-failure).
It does not establish a particular customer's diagnosis, a full septic tank,
or which service will fix it. Amber is an illustrative highlight, not a claim
about the literal material or rate of accumulation. The removed front half is
a cutaway convention, not an open pipe in use.

Dr. Plants contributes a small causal progression here: recognizable question,
visible complication, observable change, useful next action. The sports reference
contributes action readability and timed labels. Neither is being promoted into
a general application preset from one example.

Exact draft copy, production paths, visual review, and remaining work are in the
