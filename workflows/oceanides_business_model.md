# Oceanides — Business Model & Profitability Notes

> Captured 2026-07-20 from Neil's briefing. This is the economic context behind the
> Kuya Marvin brand ([oceanides_kuya_marvin_brand.md](oceanides_kuya_marvin_brand.md)).
> Legal entity: **Oceanides Plumbing Siphoning & Pest Management Services OPC**.
> Target market: **Metro Manila only.**

## The model (asset-light middleman with a compliance moat)

- Walang sariling truck. Kapag may booking, ibinabato sa kakilalang siphoning crews
  (may truck), pero **resibo at papel ng Oceanides ang ginagamit** — kumpleto sa
  BIR, SEC, DENR, at may partner na waste-management facility para sa legal na tapunan.
- **Ang moat ay ang PAPEL, hindi ang truck.** Ang crews ay hindi makaka-issue ng
  DENR-compliance certificate; si Oceanides lang.

## Unit economics (per Neil, 2026-07-20)

| Product | Presyo | Cost | Gross | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Siphoning, walang cert (residential) | ₱4,000/load, 30-day warranty | ₱2,500–3,000/load sa crew | **~₱1,000–1,500/load** | 1 load = 4 cubic / 4,000 L |
| Siphoning + DENR certificate (companies) | ₱12,000–15,000/load (tatawad si client) | same crew cost | **~₱9,000–12,000/load** | Mostly companies, minimum 5–10 loads bawat avail |
| Lusong / manual cleaning | ₱15,000–50,000+ (laki/dami ng tank) | 30% sa crew | **70% sa company** | Pinakamataas na absolute margin |

Rule of thumb: **1 certified B2B load ≈ 7–8 residential loads** ang gross profit;
**1 lusong ≈ 10–20 residential loads.**

## The poaching (sulot) problem

Ang subcontracted crews ay dumederekta sa client/contractor para ipa-cancel ang
Oceanides booking at kunin ang trabaho sa mas mababang rate — pati follow-on services
(lusong). Kaya si **Marvin ang field officer**: nagsa-site visit (angkas ₱200–300/trip)
para mag-septic check at bantayan ang crew sa lokasyon. Structural na sagot (hindi lang
bantay): poaching risk is inversely proportional sa pangangailangan ng client sa
certificate — ang B2B/compliance segment ay hindi masusulot.

## Neil/Zam comp — commission-only proposal (2026-07-20, PENDING Neil decision)

**Proposal ni Neil:** itaas ang residential ₱4,000 → ₱5,000/load standard; sa bawat
byahe, tig-₱500 si Neil at Zam (₱1,000 total kada load); walang salary/draw — puro
commission na sila.

**Agent assessment:**

- ✅ **ENDORSED ang core instinct** — commission-only para kay Neil+Zam ay tama para
  sa runway: zero fixed nut, sahod lumalabas lang kapag may kita. Ito ang tamang
  startup posture.
- ❌ **HINDI endorsed ang funding source (₱5k residential sticker):**
  1. Kontra sa same-day approved decision ("Pricing: HINDI itinaas ang sticker").
  2. Ang math ay zero-sum: sa ₱5k, gross ₱2,000–2,500 − ₱1,000 override = ₱1,000–1,500
     sa company — EKSAKTONG pareho ng ₱4k ngayon. Ang taas-presyo ay pambayad lang sa
     override; walang napupunta sa company.
  3. **Pinapalakas ang sulot problem:** lumalaki ang gap vs direct-sa-crew (~₱2.5–3k)
     mula ~₱1–1.5k papuntang ~₱2–2.5k — mas malakas ang tukso ng client na dumiretso.
     Ang residential ay ang price-shopping, poachable segment.
  4. **Incentive misalignment:** ang flat ₱500/truckload ay nagbabayad sa VOLUME ng
     byahe (residential-heavy) — pero ang approved strategy ay "compliance business":
     ang pera ay nasa B2B certified + lusong. Ang comp ang dapat tumuro doon.
- **Counter-structure (commission-only pa rin, pero naka-tier kung nasaan ang margin):**
  | Product | Override (Neil + Zam, tig-) | Naiiwan sa company |
  |---|---|---|
  | Residential ₱4,000 (unchanged) | ₱250 each | ₱500–1,000/load |
  | Certified B2B load | ₱1,000 each | ₱7,000–10,000/load |
  | Lusong | 5% each (mirror ng kay Marvin) | ~60% pa rin |
  Sample: isang 5-load B2B contract = ₱5,000 kay Neil at ₱5,000 kay Zam; 20 residential
  loads = tig-₱5,000 din — pero 1 contract vs 20 byahe ang effort. Ang B2B ang
  binabayaran nang malaki, kaya doon din tututok ang atensyon nila.

## Strategy decisions (2026-07-20 CMO session — APPROVED per Neil)

**Core repositioning: compliance business, hindi siphoning business.** Ang marketing
firepower ay nakatutok sa B2B/DENR-certified segment (un-poachable, ₱9–12k/load);
ang residential ay lead-gen papunta sa lusong, hindi ang produkto.

**Pricing: HINDI itinaas ang sticker.** Residential stays ₱4,000 (warranty valid
only through Oceanides — direct-sa-crew = walang resibo/habol/legal na tapunan).
Certified: ₱12k floor, tawad ay sinasagot ng tier ("₱15k one-time; ₱12k/load kapag
annual maintenance contract"), hindi ng discount.

**Offer ladder (ascension):**
1. HOOK — "Libreng Septic Health Check ni Kuya Marvin" (photo report; reciprocity +
   authority; lehitimong dahilan ng site presence niya = anti-poach cover)
2. CORE — ₱4,000 siphoning w/ 30-day warranty (Oceanides-only validity)
3. UPSELL — lusong ₱15–50k, ibinebenta ng Health Check report evidence
4. FLAGSHIP — "DENR Compliance Package": certified siphoning + docs + cert +
   scheduled annual/semi-annual service, per-contract pricing, 50% downpayment
   bago ang unang byahe. Dito nakatutok ang ads + Kuya Marvin B2B content.
5. RETENTION — booking database + 2–3 yr residential reminders / B2B compliance cycle

**Crew anti-poach (carrot + stick):** maintenance contracts = predictable byahe para
sa crews; isang sulot = tanggal sa buong pipeline habambuhay.

**Marvin comp (phased para sa runway; detalye sa
[oceanides_kuya_marvin_brand.md](oceanides_kuya_marvin_brand.md) + final agreement):**
Phase 0 ₱500/working day + incentives from day 1, weekly GCash; Phase 1 (₱30k gross
collected o unang B2B downpayment) base ₱15k/month + ₱1,500 likeness fee; Day-90
review. Day-14 checkpoint ay DELIVERABLES (pages live, content, canvassing, booked
Health Checks) — hindi revenue; ang revenue checkpoint ay Day 45–60.

**Offer Optimizer module:** gagamitin para i-value-stack ang DENR Compliance Package
at Health Check hook (packaging layer, pagkatapos ng economics decisions dito).
