## 1 · Food & Beverage
**Hook (S15 stat-stack, 16 ads/7 brands):** Numbers beat adjectives. Not "masarap" —
count something real. Example: *"3 tindahan. 1 lutong bahay na recipe. 500 orders kada linggo."*
**Angle (S15 + S12 pattern-interrupt, 9/5):** Your best-seller's numbers ARE the ad.
People scroll past "quality food"; they stop at a number that feels earned.
**Skeleton:** `[Bilang] na [produkto] ang nabenta namin nitong [panahon]. Walang sikreto —
[totoong dahilan]. Subukan mo ang [best-seller] — [simpleng CTA: order/punta/DM].`

## 2 · Fashion & Beauty
**Hook (S16 borrowed authority, 18/6):** Let a real customer say it. A quoted line from
a real buyer outperforms your own claim.
**Angle (S16 + S25 meme-native, 4/3):** Proof-by-association: real reviews, real photos,
worn by real people. Zero stock photos — feed-native shots win.
**Skeleton:** `"[Totoong quote ng customer]" — [pangalan/initials]. [Ilang] na ang bumili
ng [produkto]. Ikaw na ang susunod — [CTA].`

## 3 · Health & Wellness
**Hook (S1 risk-reversal headline, 9 ads, proposed):** Put the guarantee IN the headline,
not the fine print: *"Kung walang pagbabago sa [panahon], ibabalik namin ang bayad mo."*
**Angle (S1):** In a scam-scared niche, removing risk outsells promising results. Never
invent medical claims — the guarantee is the claim.
**Skeleton:** `[Pangako na may guarantee sa unang linya]. [Paano ito gumagana — 1 linya].
[Totoong proof: bilang ng clients / years]. [CTA na walang pressure: "subukan mo, walang risk"].`

## 4 · Home Services
**Hook (S12 pattern-interrupt, 9/5):** Name the ugly problem bluntly in line one —
the thing the customer is embarrassed about. *"May amoy na ba ang [problema] sa bahay mo?"*
**Angle (S28 transformation timeline — real artifacts only *(hypothesis, 2 ads)* + S15):**
Before → during → after with REAL job photos + one number ("[X] na bahay ngayong buwan").
**Skeleton:** `[Tanong na tumutusok sa problema]. [Gaano kabilis ang ayos]. [Totoong
before/after photo]. [Bilang ng na-serbisyuhan]. [CTA: "Message mo kami ngayon — libre ang tingin."]`

## 5 · Professional Services
**Hook (S2 authority portrait, 15/4):** YOU are the proof. Face + one earned credential
in the first line. People hire people, not logos.
**Angle (S2 + S16):** Founder-as-proof: your photo, your real numbers, one client result
with permission. No stock handshake photos.
**Skeleton:** `Ako si [pangalan], [credential na totoo]. Sa loob ng [panahon], [na-tulungan
kong bilang] na [klase ng client] na [resulta]. Kung [pain ng client], usap tayo — [CTA].`

## 6 · Online Store (E-commerce)
**Hook (S15 + S4 offer-stack checklist, 6/5):** Stack what they GET as a checklist with
one anchor number: *"✅ [item] ✅ [freebie] ✅ [garantiya] — ₱[presyo] lang."*
**Angle (S4 + S27 daily-cost anchor *(hypothesis, 2 ads)*):** The offer stack IS the
creative. Make the value stack visibly larger than the price.
**Skeleton:** `[Produkto] + [freebie] + [free shipping/garantiya] = [kabuuang halaga].
Ngayon: [presyo]. [Totoong bilang ng nabenta/reviews]. [CTA: Order na — COD available.]`

## 7 · Coaching & Education
**Hook (S10 value-first magnet, 6/5):** Give the first lesson FREE in the ad itself.
Teach one real thing in 3 lines — the ad that teaches, sells.
**Angle (S10 + S2):** Generosity-first: free value builds the authority that sells the
paid program. (Exactly what Hormozi runs — F03/F06 magnet funnels.)
**Skeleton:** `[Isang totoong tip — buo, hindi teaser]. Gusto mo ng [buong version/libreng
guide]? [CTA]. [Isang linya kung sino ka at bakit ka dapat pakinggan — S2].`

## 8 · Real Estate
**Hook (S15 stat-stack):** Specificity sells property: *"[sqm], [lokasyon], [payment terms]
— [presyo/buwan]."* Vague "dream home" ads lose to exact numbers.
**Angle (S15 + S16 borrowed authority):** Numbers + trust markers (licensed broker no.,
developer name, actual turnover dates). This niche is fraud-scared — every claim checkable.
**Skeleton:** `[Lokasyon]: [sqm] / [beds] / ₱[monthly] kada buwan. [Turnover date].
[License/developer proof]. [CTA: "Free tripping — book mo dito."]`

## 9 · Other (general)
**Hook (S12 pattern-interrupt):** Open with the customer's exact words for their problem —
the sentence they say to themselves at 3AM. If your first line could fit any business, rewrite it.
**Angle (S1 + S4 + S15 — the corroborated core trio):** Risk-reversal + offer stack +
one real number. The universal direct-response spine.
**Skeleton:** `[Pain sa mismong salita ng customer]. [Ano ang inaalok mo — 1 linya].
[Isang totoong numero bilang proof]. [Garantiya kung kaya mo]. [Isang malinaw na CTA].`
