# Oceanides — 30-Day Organic Content Calendar

**Audience:** Homeowners (emergency barado/overflow + preventive sipsip), light commercial. Metro Manila · Calabarzon · Central Luzon.
**Register:** Modern Taglish (how Filipinos actually talk — NO deep Tagalog; deep Tagalog reads as AI).
**Format:** 4:5 (1080×1350). Contact + logo baked on EVERY post (core theft-proofing).
**Goal:** Balanced trust + leads. Front-load trust Week 1 (cold audience), ramp offers Weeks 2–4.

## Pillars → pipeline
| Pillar | What | Pipeline |
|---|---|---|
| **EDU** | Education / symptom / how-it-works | **Codex** art + overlay |
| **LEGIT** | SEC/DENR/BIR, anti-scam | Codex/graphic + overlay |
| **OFFER** | Promo, FREE consultation, urgency | Codex art + overlay |
| **TRUST** | 24/7, areas, FAQ, walang hidden charges, reviews | Codex/graphic + overlay |
| **PROOF** | Before/After, truck, job (REAL photo) | **Template composite** + tiled watermark |

## Asset reality (drives the mix)
- Only **1 real before/after pair** + **unbranded truck photos** (digital-branded), **no crew photos**, few reviews.
- So the mix leans on **codex-producible** pillars (EDU/LEGIT/OFFER/TRUST) and uses the scarce real photos sparingly. Get crew to snap 2–3 phone photos per job → grows the PROOF library over time.
- **Mix:** EDU 9 · OFFER 6 · LEGIT 5 · TRUST 5 · PROOF 5.

## Theft-proofing rule
Core (baked numbers + logo) = every post. Heavy tiled watermark = PROOF + OFFER posts only.
