This Agreement is made between MARVIN BALABA ("Talent") and OCEANIDES PLUMBING
SIPHONING & PEST MANAGEMENT SERVICES OPC ("Company"), effective on the date of the
Talent's reply of agreement to this email.

1. **What the Talent agrees to.** The Talent allows the Company to use his name, the
   nickname "Kuya Marvin", his photographs, image, likeness, and voice in marketing
   content for Oceanides septic tank and siphoning services — including social media
   pages (Facebook, TikTok, and similar), advertisements, and related marketing
   materials.
2. **AI-generated content.** The Talent agrees that the Company may create a
   digital/AI version of his likeness and voice from reference photos he provides,
   and may publish AI-generated videos and images using that likeness. The Company
   commits that: (a) AI-generated content will be labeled according to platform
   rules; (b) the Talent's likeness will only be used for content about Oceanides'
   business; and (c) his likeness will never be used for statements that are false,
   misleading, defamatory, or illegal.
3. **Review and objection.** The Talent may ask to see any published content using
   his likeness. If he reasonably objects to a specific post or video, the Company
   will take it down within a reasonable time.

   Marvin ng Oceanides" brand name are owned by the Company. The Talent keeps
   ownership of his personal identity and his own personal accounts.

   receive a likeness fee of ₱1,500 per month, starting when Phase 1 of the work
   arrangement begins, and continuing while this Agreement is active. This likeness
   fee is separate from the Talent's compensation for field work.

   ends it with thirty (30) days written notice (text or email counts). After it
   ends, the Company will stop making NEW content with the Talent's likeness. For
   content already published before the end date: **[X] It may remain published**
   (with the Talent's Section 3 objection right intact).
7. **Governing law.** Republic of the Philippines.
