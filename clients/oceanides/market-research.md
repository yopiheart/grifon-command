# Oceanides — Who You Sell To (Market Research + Framework Verification)

*Built with the Grifon CMO stack: Cialdini's 7 Principles · Sabri Suby's $100M Copywriting System · MrBeast's Viral Playbook · Steve Jobs' work-backwards filter. This doc names WHERE each framework is used so you can verify it's actually applied — not just claimed.*

---

## 1. Primary avatar — "Homeowner Henry & Helen" (PH)

- **Who:** 30–55, may sariling bahay sa **Metro Manila / Cavite-Laguna-Batangas / Bulacan-Pampanga**. Working-class to lower-middle. Nasa phone (FB/Messenger) 90% ng oras.
- **Bleeding neck:** *"Barado / umaapaw / mabaho ang CR — at kailangan ko `to ayusin AGAD, pero natatakot akong malokohin."*
- **3AM thought:** *"Baka scam `yang malabanan na `yan. Baka sobrang mahal, o palpak, o may hidden charges pagkatapos maghukay."*
- **#1 objection:** **Scam / fly-by-night / hidden charges.** (Ito ang buong dahilan kung bakit *trust* ang core ng lahat ng content.)
- **Villain:** Ang fly-by-night na malabanan (personal FB account, walang permit) + ang DIY-Muriatic-acid na hindi umuubra.
- **Emergency vs maintenance:** 70% emergency (barado/overflow, mabilis magdesisyon), 30% preventive (pa-siphon kada 3–5 taon).

## 2. Secondary avatar — "Property Manager / SME Paolo"

- Carinderia, apartment/boarding house, subdivision, contractor. **Mas malaking ticket + recurring.** Mas mahaba ang desisyon, may decision-maker. Content: reliability, receipts/legit, service contract.

## 3. Framework application (verification map)

### Cialdini's 7 Principles — saan ginamit
| Principle | Saan sa Oceanides content |
| --- | --- |
| **Authority** | SEC-registered corporation + DENR + BIR badges sa BAWAT post; "bakit SEC-registered ang piliin" post |
| **Social Proof** | Before/After job proof; before/after `nakakadiri = totoong trabaho. (Testimonial posts pending real reviews — no fake proof rule) |
| **Scarcity / Urgency** | Tag-ulan prep ("bago bumaha"); promo posts; 24/7 + 30–60 min response |
| **Reciprocity** | Educational posts (paano gumagana, 5 senyales, hindi-itapon) = libreng halaga muna; **libreng check-up** = foot-in-the-door |
| **Liking** | Premium Taglish (hindi malalim = hindi AI/robot); "malapit lang kami"; 24/7 empathy |
| **Commitment/Consistency** | Libreng check-up → maliit na "yes" → mas madaling mag-book ng full service |
| **Unity** | "Para sa mga may-bahay sa Metro Manila, Cavite & Bulacan" (niche callout = same-tribe) |

### Sabri Suby — $100M offer + copy
- **Dream outcome:** Malinis, walang baho, walang barado — **at walang gulat sa presyo.**
- **Perceived likelihood:** SEC-registered proof + before/after + permits (bakit ito gagana para sa'yo).
- **Time delay:** Same-day, 30–60 min response.
- **Effort/sacrifice:** Isang tawag / DM lang.
- **Guarantee:** **Walang hidden charges — presyo agad bago simulan.**
- **Real urgency:** Tag-ulan season, promo (walang fake countdown).
- **Consumption precedes conversion:** Madaling basahin, maikli, Taglish, education-first — kaya nabasa bago nag-convert.

### MrBeast — hooks + thumbnails
- **Hooks:** "BARADO NA NAMAN?", "5 SENYALES...", "BAKIT MABAHO..." — named pain + curiosity gap, hindi brand name ang una.
- **Thumbnail rules applied:** 3–5 malalaking salita, gold (#F5B301) + white sa navy, isang focal point, high contrast, condensed bold (Anton). **Never presyo sa creative.**

### Steve Jobs — work-backwards
- Nagsimula sa **panic moment ng homeowner** (barado, 3AM, takot sa presyo) → dun hinango ang content: proof-first, contact baked-in, "walang hidden charges." **Hindi** nagsimula sa "anong cool na design" — customer experience muna, design pangalawa.

## 4. Content pillars → avatar psychology
1. **PROOF/LEGIT** → kills the #1 objection (scam fear). *Authority.*
2. **BEFORE/AFTER** → visceral proof of real work. *Social Proof.*
3. **EDUCATION** → positions as expert + reciprocity. *Reciprocity + Authority.*
4. **OFFER/URGENCY** → captures the ready-to-buy. *Scarcity + Commitment.*
5. **TRUST** → 24/7, areas, walang hidden charges. *Liking + Guarantee.*

## 5. Open (para sa susunod)
- **Real testimonials** — kailangan bago gumawa ng review posts (no fake proof).
- **Target-market validation** — kumpirmahin kay client: aling barangay/segment ang pinaka-maraming tawag (para i-sharpen ang niche callout + ad targeting).
- **Ad targeting** (kung mag-ads): homeowners 28–55, MM/Cavite/Bulacan, interests: home improvement, real estate, may-ari ng bahay.
