# Oceanides conversation coaching proposal

Revision: **CM-2026-10-19-r1**. Prepared using `grifon-cmo` at Neil's request.
**Proposed only. No new prompt, opening, model, voice or speech setting applied.**

The approved Builder baseline and free interruption/pause protocol stay in

## Recommendation and success measure

Answer the caller's question, acknowledge a stated concern, then offer one
relevant, optional next step when they want to continue. Calm, useful answers
fit the Oceanides brand better than a close after every turn. Preserve the
approved Michael greeting and Altair voice; do not import ad-hook rules into
the greeting, force emotion tags or add artificial response delays.

The first conversational stage is inquiry to willingness to continue with a
human-reviewed quote. The later commercial measure is verified,
customer-accepted bookings per eligible inquiry. The preview has no dispatch
or callback integration and cannot deliver or measure those bookings.
Prompt quality cannot remove the known operational dependency: Neil/Zam must
check the final price and crew slot with Boss Jay, then obtain customer acceptance.

The skill's clarity, Liking and voluntary Commitment principles are design
lenses, not proof of lift. The static/video swipe files contain no corroborated
Oceanides voice-call conversion experiment. Their ad patterns do not establish
that these spoken lines work. No numeric uplift or success probability is
supported by the present evidence; a synthetic voice test cannot guarantee sales.

## Exact proposed Instructions addition, not applied

Append this paragraph to the approved Instructions, immediately before
"Known synthetic lead". All existing facts and capability boundaries remain:

```text
Help the caller make an informed next-step decision. Answer their question first, briefly acknowledge a stated concern, and explain only the relevant confirmed facts. When the caller has finished and wants to continue, offer one clear, optional next step; ask only for missing information. Do not turn every reply into a pitch or push past hesitation or refusal. In this preview, the next step is discussion or readback, never a claimed booking, saved request or completed handoff.
```

AGENTS.md requires approval of this specific new prompt text before applying it.
The user's request to use the CMO skill authorizes this review/proposal, not an
unseen prompt revision. The paragraph does not authorize any test, spend,
publication, production action or new capability.

Do not re-ask known city/address/service details. Ask one missing question at a
time, only after the caller finishes. If they decline, stop selling. Unknown
policies, final price and crew availability stay with Neil/Zam; no new proof,
discount, guarantee, service diagnosis, urgency or response-time promise is added.

## Evaluation after baseline voice checks

The current free scripts can reveal relevant replies, correct price units,
unnecessary questions, pressure and natural delivery. Do not add an objection
probe or change those scripts while running that baseline. Review separately
before applying this proposal or approving a sales-conversation test.

Later, with a working handoff and separately authorized real pilot, compare
eligible inquiries and verified accepted bookings, recording lead source,
crew availability, approval delay and reasons for no booking. Report raw counts
and uncertainty. Do not count friendly speech, completed calls, synthetic
readbacks or stated willingness as confirmed bookings or conversion lift.
