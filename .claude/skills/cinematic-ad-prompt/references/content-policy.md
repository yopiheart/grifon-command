# Content-Policy-Safe Construction

The goal: write scenes that achieve full cinematic impact while never *needing* to depict prohibited content. This is a craft constraint, not a limitation — the substitutions below usually read as *more* cinematic, not less. Build the scene safe from the start rather than describing something risky and hoping it passes the model's filter (a refused or auto-edited generation wastes credits — and for Grifon prep, credits are real money).

## The principle

Don't depict the harm; depict the energy, the aftermath, the threat, or the scale. A standoff is more tense than a massacre. An evacuated city is eerier than a crowded one in panic. A roar is more powerful than a kill.

## Substitution table

| Avoid | Use instead |
|---|---|
| Civilians in panic, crowds fleeing under debris | An **evacuated** / empty city; abandoned streets; a lone figure for scale |
| Military weapons firing into buildings / at people | **Energy-discharge standoffs**, searchlights sweeping, charged auras, shockwaves with no muzzle fire |
| Creatures physically tearing into each other / gore | A **grapple/standoff**, roars, near-misses, circling, an energy clash; combat contained |
| Destruction with people in harm's way | Destruction in **uninhabited terrain** — glaciers, deserts, ruins, open sea, evacuated zones |
| Realistic guns as the focus | Stylized/fantasy implements, slung-not-fired weapons, the weapon as silhouette/prop only |
| Blood, wounds, death | Impact light, dust, debris, buckling and collapse, a silhouette falling out of frame |
| Real, named public figures | Original/anonymous characters |
| Real brand logos (not the user's own) | Original or generalized branding (the user's own product is the point and stays) |

## The confirmed benchmark

The reference safe scene: **a single original creature in a generalized monument/amphitheatre, in daytime, no weapons present, performing expressive action (rising, roaring, spreading wings).** One creature, original design, generalized location, daylight, expressive rather than violent action. When a scene drifts risky, pull it back toward this shape.

## Minors — hard rule

Never write romantic, sexual, or suggestive content involving or directed at minors, and never anything that sexualizes a young-presenting character. Keep any scene involving children wholesome and age-appropriate. Non-negotiable; overrides any stylistic goal.

## Creatures and combat — keep it contained

- One or two **original** creatures, explicitly "not based on any franchise".
- Give the fight a **containment rule** that also grounds the physics: "stays at the sea surface", "never flies into the air", "boss scale locked ~2.5 human-heights, not kaiju-giant". This simultaneously improves coherence and avoids escalation.
- Favor **expressive action** (rising, roaring, breaching, spreading wings, a head snapping toward the hero) over depicted injury.

## Scale and stakes without harm

Tiny figures diving clear of *collapsing terrain* (not being crushed), a war-horn over an *empty* field, an army *scrambling* across a frozen valley as a titan tears free of a glacier — the danger is environmental and the figures are reacting, not victims. Snow plumes, glowing runes, splintering ice carry the chaos.

## Direct-response ad guardrails (Grifon)

Beyond the model's content policy, Grifon ads have a **truthfulness** standard — these protect Neil's account on Meta/TikTok and Grifon's trust-as-core-asset doctrine:

- **No fabricated proof.** Don't render fake before/after results, invented statistics on screen, fake reviewer counts, or staged "as seen on" badges. A transformation shot must be plausibly generic, not a fake clinical claim.
- **No fake urgency/scarcity.** No invented countdown timers, "only 3 left" overlays, or false "ending tonight" text unless it is literally true.
- **No real third-party brand logos / real public figures** as implied endorsers.
- **Health/finance claims:** keep visuals suggestive of feeling/outcome (energy, confidence, calm), never a guaranteed medical/financial result on screen.
- **No price on screen** (Grifon house rule — CTA only; see `direct-response.md`).

When a request pushes toward any of these, build the honest version (real mechanism, real outcome, real offer) — it converts better and survives ad review.

## Quick pre-flight

Before delivering any prompt with conflict, creatures, crowds, weapons, destruction, young characters, OR direct-response claims, confirm:
- [ ] No civilians depicted in panic/harm; crowds evacuated or absent.
- [ ] No weapons firing at people/buildings; threat is energy/searchlight/silhouette.
- [ ] No creature/person gore; combat is grapple/standoff/roar, contained.
- [ ] Destruction is in uninhabited/evacuated terrain.
- [ ] Creatures original (not franchise IP); no real public figures; no real third-party brand logos.
- [ ] Nothing involving minors is anything but wholesome and age-appropriate.
- [ ] **DR:** no fabricated proof, fake urgency/scarcity, fake endorsements, on-screen medical/financial guarantees, or price.

If a checkbox fails, apply the substitution table / build the honest version before writing.
