// ACT IV · WHAT TO FIX OR REMOVE

Precision is the credibility device.

L9's whole lesson: the GOATs are believed because their numbers are exact (“$7.8 billion in 1067 niches”). Ours wobble in three places:

  • Price-anchor chaos — FIXED. Was four prices on one page (₱497 / ₱997 / ₱2,497). Now: the dead ₱497 bar is gone, marquee + metrics read from ₱997/mo (Starter, verified against PLAN_CATALOG), and the ₱2,497 Pro story lives only in the OfferStack — the F05 shape.
  • “100+ businesses” — FIXED to “20+”, the number the code itself documents as the real 2025 client count. Under THE NUMBERS DON'T LIE, a real 20+ beats an inflatable 100+.
  • Vague hero trust row — FIXED. “Early Filipino business owners” is now “20+ Filipino business owners,” mirroring the metrics band. Upgrade the figure as the real count grows.
  • The founding-offer decision — RESOLVED by audit (Act I): ₱497 was never in the checkout catalog, so the bar is disabled until a true promo exists.

// ACT V · THE L1 UNLOCK AUDIT (RUN-SHEET 05)

Our thank-you moment leaks.

L1 — the post-opt-in moment sells exactly ONE next step — is the most corroborated pattern in the swipe file (6 of 6 funnels). Our equivalent peak is the screen where the free ad plan finishes. Code-walked end to end on 07-29. Verdict: FAIL, with receipts — and one genuinely good asset to build on.

The census: 17 clickables. 1 money path.

L1 FAIL

At the exact moment Hormozi shows one video and one button, our completion screen offers seventeen clickable elements across ~ten destinations — tabs, downloads, back-buttons, a restart, an NPS card — and exactly one path toward money (GENERATE VIDEO NOW >), styled like a free next step. No price, no plan name, no “1 of 1 free workflows used,” no watermark disclosure anywhere on the screen.

The five sharpest leaks

  • The perfect nudge is dead code. A Taglish line written for exactly this moment (“Gawa mo na ang free ad plan mo — i-render na natin…”) exists in config/upgradeNudge.ts but is never rendered anywhere — it survives only as a boolean powering a bell-dot on the Dashboard.
  • A banner hijacks the peak. One-shot users see “Run Market Research First” on the completion screen — routing them away from their finished ad at maximum attention.
  • The free exit out-muscles the paid one. EXPORT_BUNDLE (.ZIP) hands over everything, fully satisfying, co-equal in the layout with the money CTA.
  • Paying destroys the ad. Both paywall exits (pricing page, ₱499 top-up) lose all wizard state — a user who pays comes back to an empty dashboard, not to the ad they just bought.
  • Watermark leak. The image lightbox’s download button serves the raw, un-watermarked asset — the free tier’s only passive friction, gone in one click.

The bridge is built

SHIPPED · 07-30

LockedVideoPreview was already the right L1 mechanic — the user’s own ad, blurred behind a gold lock. The bridge slice around it is now live on the branch: the completion screen states its state (“1/1 free workflow used · exports watermarked”), the dead Taglish nudge finally renders at its moment, the gold CTA names its price (I-RENDER ANG VIDEO — STARTER ₱997), the free export is demoted to a ghost button, the research banner no longer hijacks the peak, and the lightbox watermark leak is plugged.

Resume routing — also shipped (07-30): the finished ad is now always reachable again — a one-click “Balikan ang ad mo” card on the Dashboard, both paywall exits stash a return-to-ad intent, and the post-payment screen offers BALIK SA AD MO — I-RENDER NA when a finished run exists. The critic pass also surfaced the next revenue bug: a Free user who BUYS the ₱499 video credit still hits the paywall (no credit check in the gate) — queued in workflows/post_preview_bridge.md.
