// What this establishes

An editable production starting point.

Procedural geometry, materials, lights and four cameras are saved in the Blender file. No downloaded textures or external image dependencies. The three stills received independent composition review.

Still review axis: trust through coherent physical staging. No numerical ranking and no claim that this beats the Higgsfield reference.

// What remains a creative decision

The finished look and real proof.

This rough set is not a final cinematic render or an ad generated by Grifon. Human performance, approved coffee identity, richer materials, sound and actual Grifon UGC/cinematic exports are outside this pilot.

The next decision is whether this room and these camera relationships suit the film. Final runtime and timed storyboard remain open.
