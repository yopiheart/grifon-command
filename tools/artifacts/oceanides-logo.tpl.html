Oceanides · brand marks

One brand.
Two jobs.

Neil's new mark is the face of the brand. It is built for scale and for dark grounds. It does not survive 40px or a CMYK press — so a second, plainer mark does that work. This is not a competition between them; it is a division of labour.

2026-09-03 · roles settled

Primary — Neil's mark

Facebook profile and cover, video endcards, signage, anything large, anything on a dark ground. This is what people should recognise as Oceanides.

Where it stops working, measured

1254×1254 PNG, 149,907 distinct colours, and no alpha channel — the near-black ground is baked in (#00000A at all four corners).

A circle mask handles the ground fine at 80px and up. At 40px it turns to mush — and 40px is exactly what the funnel footer renders, and close to what a business card gets. The glow and gradients also have nowhere to go in CMYK: on matte lamination they go muddy. That is the whole reason the second mark exists.

Print & small — the plain mark

Business card, favicon, one-colour embroidery on a uniform, rubber stamp, anything under about 24mm. Same brand, stripped to what survives.

What was measured, not guessed

QuestionAnswer
Does the navy match the palette? Yes. Logo #003060 vs surface #0E2C5E = RGB distance 14.7. Near identical.
Does amber match? No. 0 of 99,856 pixels are warm. Zero. Amber is a signage decision (DESIGN.md:93), never derived from the mark.
Was it low quality? Yes. 18,832 distinct colours in a 316px file. A clean logo has a few hundred.
Was there a vector master? No. public/logo.svg is a stub rendering the word "LOGO". Every use everywhere was a resample of one small JPEG.

What is still open

1 · Typeface settled — Archivo Expanded ExtraBold

Your board is filed at brand/type-inspo/, renamed by pairing. Six came in. Four are out on licence or register: Switzer, Vanguard, Athelas, Balboa and Epic Pro are paid or unreachable, and a script or handwriting face is wrong for a company whose whole pitch is having its papers in order.

Of what was left, the winner is the Archivo half of your slide 05 — so your board and my placeholder landed on the same family independently.

It was picked by measuring, not taste: all six set as OCEANIDES at 50px, at 19px reversed on navy, and at 11px — the real ~5pt card size. Archivo Expanded holds its counters there. Abril Fatface, the other half of that same slide, closes up: a Didone's thin strokes vanish at 5pt and at truck distance. Expanded also reads wider, and width is what makes a service company look established rather than new. Licence is SIL OFL — free for commercial use, logos included, and the font is committed at brand/fonts/ so nothing depends on a CDN.

2 · Amber is not in the mark, on purpose

Your accent has zero basis in the logo. That may be exactly right — amber earns its place on signage and CTAs by stopping the scroll against a sea of blue, which is a different job from a mark. Adding a warm accent to the shield is your call to make after seeing it, not something a redraw should smuggle in.
