# Grifon Pricing Ladder — persona-facing summary (shared)

Shared reference for the `grifon-cmo` and `grifon-creative-director` skills. It exists so the
pricing ladder lives in **one** place instead of drifting across two SKILL.md copies. Re-read this
before writing any tier / price / feature / CTA copy or any creative that names a tier.

**Canonical source (these win on any conflict — this summary can lag):**
[`workflows/pricing_unit_economics.md`](../../../workflows/pricing_unit_economics.md) §3 +
[`config/planFeatures.ts`](../../../config/planFeatures.ts). Re-read them before pitching tiers,
prices, features, or CTAs. (This summary exists because the retired "Creator" name once drifted —
don't let it drift again.)

`PlanId = 'free' | 'starter' | 'pro' | 'business'`. **Autopilot is invite-only — NOT a public card,
NOT a checkout PlanId.**

| Tier | Price/mo (annual) | Finished ads/mo | Scene cap | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | ₱0 | 0 (full *plan*, watermarked, no video) | — | cold signups (the Free consult = our CAC) |
| **Starter** | ₱997 | 1 (auto-edit only, no editor) | ≤4 | trial door + price anchor → Pro |
| **Pro** ⭐ | ₱2,497 (₱2,081 annual) | 8 (AI editor, Director mode, caption packs) | ≤4 | THE money tier |
| **Business** | ₱4,997 (₱4,164 annual) | 15 + Commercial/Spec metered, cinematic/4K studio, weekly call | ≤6 (8 spec) | scaling / agencies |
| **Autopilot** | from ₱29,997 · invite-only | done-for-you | — | apply-only (P1M MRR lives here) |

**Top-ups (overage = highest-margin revenue):** ₱499 single ad (also Free's no-commitment door +
Starter overage) · ₱2,250 5-pack (₱450/ad) · ~₱1,200 cinematic/Spec ad.

## Shared guardrails (apply on every tier/price/CTA surface)

- **DO NOT pitch / feature (sold-but-stripped 2026-06-19, not built):** brand profiles · team seats ·
  white-label exports.
- **There is NO "VIP" tier.** Never put "VIP" on a card, lockup, badge, or creative.
- **CTA by temperature:** cold → "Try It Free" · warm upsell → "Upgrade to Pro" · Autopilot →
  "Apply for Autopilot".
- **Never show price in thumbnails / ad creatives.** (Price is fine on landing / pricing pages.)
