## Review criteria and remaining limits

Planning success means the viewer can follow invitation → playful one-upmanship → shared movement → product hero; both cast roles remain distinct; the speaker's music role is legible; and every source claim stays separate from authored direction. Failures to catch later include gestures too fast to read, a camera angle that swaps the friends, a disappearing phone or drifting speaker, unconvincing rising action, and a hero that invents the real product's design.

The written timeline and continuity were checked against this plan. No visual motion, contact, audio mix, render quality, identity fidelity, or provider compatibility has been validated. The only deliberate temporal ellipsis is ordinary editorial coverage across the playful gestures; the rise is visible in S06, and no hidden product handoff is required.
