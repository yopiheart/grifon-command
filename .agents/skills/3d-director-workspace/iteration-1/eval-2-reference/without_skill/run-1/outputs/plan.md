## Blocking and continuity

Keep A left and B right throughout. Place the speaker on one fixed table mark between them. Draw the interaction axis through the two performers; shoot the close-ups and over-shoulder from one side so their glances remain consistent. The high-angle insert can be neutral, but return to the established side afterward. Frame the conductor hands above and beside the product, never across its main face. Neither performer transfers or operates the speaker, avoiding unsupported control placement and complicated hand contact.

Use one soft daylight direction across every angle, with a restrained background and enough separation for the final product silhouette.
